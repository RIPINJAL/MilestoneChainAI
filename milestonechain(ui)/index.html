The Problem

⚠️

Misuse of Investor Funds

Traditional crowdfunding lacks accountability. Creators can misuse funds without consequences or proper oversight.

🔒

Lack of Transparency

Investors have no visibility into how their money is being spent or real-time project progress updates.

❌

No Milestone Verification

High failure rates due to poor planning, unrealistic goals, and lack of independent verification mechanisms.

Our Solution

🔗

Blockchain Escrow

Funds locked in smart contracts and released only when milestones are verified by independent validators.

🤖

AI-Based Risk Scoring

Machine learning analyzes project feasibility, creator history, milestone quality, and submission credibility.

✅

Multi-Stakeholder Validation

Independent validators review and approve milestones before fund release, ensuring accountability.
